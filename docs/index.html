of scrolling.

A tiny browser extension that catches you right before the endless scroll on YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels starts. Not a prison, just a pause.

How it works

You reach for a Short. It reaches back.

You tap a Short or a Reel

Out of habit, from the home page, a search, a link a friend sent. It doesn't matter how you got there.

"Not today."

Instead of the feed you get a calm stop page, how many times you've tried today, and one question: what were you about to do?

Still want it? Fine.

Wait 10 seconds and watch just that one, in the normal player. No swipe, no "next", no feed after it.

What it touches

Only the scroll. Nothing else.

Stops

  • YouTube Shorts, and the Shorts shelves, sidebar tab and search results
  • Instagram Reels, the feed and single reels
  • Instagram Explore, plus the Reels and Explore buttons

Leaves alone

  • Normal YouTube videos, subscriptions and playlists
  • Your Instagram feed, posts, profiles and DMs
  • Every other website

Install

Get it running in a minute.

The Edge Add-ons and Chrome Web Store listings are on their way. Until then you can install it straight from GitHub:

  1. Download the latest so-tired-of-scrolling-*.zip from Releases and unzip it.
  2. Open edge://extensions or chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.

Questions

Fair questions.

Why not just block it completely?

Because a wall you resent gets uninstalled. The urge to open Shorts lasts a few seconds; a pause and a counter are usually enough to break it, and when you genuinely want one video, you can still watch it, without the feed that follows.

What data does it collect?

None. It keeps two numbers in your browser, today's date and today's try count, and makes no network requests of its own. Read the privacy policy or the code.

Does it work on my phone?

Only in a desktop browser for now. The YouTube and Instagram apps aren't browsers, so no extension can reach them. On Android, Digital Wellbeing → App timers is the closest thing.

YouTube changed something and Shorts are back.

YouTube and Instagram change their pages often. Open an issue with a screenshot and it'll get fixed.
