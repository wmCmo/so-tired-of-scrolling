Privacy policy

I'm so tired of scrolling collects nothing.

  • It stores exactly two values, in your browser only (chrome.storage.local): today's date and how many times you've hit the stop page today. They never leave your device, and the count resets each day.
  • It makes no network requests of its own: no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no ads.
  • It only runs on youtube.com and instagram.com, to recognise Shorts, Reels and Explore pages and hide the links to them. It doesn't read or store what you watch, search or post.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes the stored count.

The code is open source, so you can check all of the above: github.com/wmCmo/so-tired-of-scrolling. Questions: open an issue.
